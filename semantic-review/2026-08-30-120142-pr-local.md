## Idempotency rows can redirect replay to another valid artifact

**High · confirmed.** `_lookup_idempotency` in `src/ecatsl/artifact_repository.py` verifies that `result_artifact_ids_json` is a non-empty unique string list and loads every reference through the closed registry. Handlers then validate only model type—`persist_artifact`, for example, returns `_expect(replay[0], type(artifact))`—without requiring the returned identity to equal the requested artifact or requiring the operation's exact result count.

A direct corruption probe persisted two distinct `AcceptancePolicy` artifacts, replaced the first request's result list with the second policy's ID, and replayed the unchanged first request. Replay returned the second policy (`redirected=True`) instead of raising `ImmutableArtifactError`; single-result handlers also ignore valid trailing IDs after `replay[0]`.

Required outcome 6 therefore remains incomplete. Single-artifact operations should require the requested artifact ID exactly; compound policy/classification operations should validate the expected candidate/decision/failure structure and reject extra, missing, reordered, or substituted IDs. `test_corrupted_idempotency_result_list_is_rejected` currently covers only malformed `{}` and needs same-type substitution and valid trailing-ID cases.

Validation otherwise remained green: the focused repository/schema command passed `25` tests, the full `tests\unit\ecatsl tests\integration\ecatsl` command passed `39` tests, and compileall over `src\ecatsl`, `tests\unit\ecatsl`, and `tests\integration\ecatsl` exited `0`. **High · confirmed — Not tested:** the green suites do not exercise semantic result-list substitution; the direct probe demonstrates the missing rejection.
