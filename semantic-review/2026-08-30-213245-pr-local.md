## Audit provenance stops at the input Evidence

Property 2 compares all provenance fields on retained Evidence: `origin`, `retrieved_at`, `source_identifier`, nullable `source_revision`, `content_identity`, and `transformation_history`. For acceptance, however, the generated `PolicyDecisionRecord` is only checked for ledger origin, timezone-aware time, a truthy identity, and fixed history. For the validation successor it checks origin, time, and history only; it never checks provenance on the acceptance successor.

This leaves `source_identifier`, `source_revision`, and exact `content_identity` unasserted for the decision and either successor. A regression that assigns an unrelated source reference or incorrect/non-deterministic identity can keep the property green while breaking the requested policy audit surface. Assert the derived source identifier and revision, computed artifact identity, and all provenance fields on both decisions and both successors. This is a test-coverage gap, not an observed implementation failure.

## Exact audit inputs are checked only in the smallest case

Acceptance asserts the exact IDs for root candidate, policy, and one Evidence; validation asserts the candidate, validation result, and policy. This includes the required Evidence or Validation_Result identity in the exercised paths.

`apply_acceptance` accepts sequences of Evidence and Counterexamples, but Property 2 fixes `counterexamples=()` and supplies one Evidence item; validation supplies no Counterexample. It therefore cannot detect a second input being dropped, duplicates being retained, or an unrelated ID being added. Generate distinct multi-item Evidence and Counterexample sequences and compare the decision against the expected sorted, de-duplicated ID set.
