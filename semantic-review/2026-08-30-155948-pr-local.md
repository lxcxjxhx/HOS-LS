The Task 2.4 acceptance policy now grants `independent_evidence` credit only to four explicitly local, reviewed producers: `repository`, `validation`, `input-tracer`, and `sast-prefilter-codeql`. ## High-level view

Acceptance separates source trust from provenance matching. The closed local-producer taxonomy is the only route to satisfying `independent_evidence`; `origin:<name>` remains a non-confirmatory selector and cannot confer static-path confirmation authority.

Policy evaluation remains a complete decision record rather than a proof claim. Validation can preserve or reduce the lifecycle state but cannot promote a candidate to confirmed.

SQLite extension migration journaling isolates ECATSL failures from the populated catalog, including rollback, restart recovery, and idempotent replay. Confirmation remains separately gated by an authorized supported adapter, complete path-bound lineage, and a non-blocking sanitizer.
